How to Play

Basic Gameplay

Choose a character, move with WASD, aim with the mouse, and use E or Space to select a skill. Click to cast. Press the same skill key again to cancel an aimed skill. Watch cooldowns, keep moving, and learn each character\'s strengths.

Important Skills to Watch

    Tips for Playing and Countering

    Use the arena space, bait out enemy cooldowns, and do not waste your skills at maximum distance. Against fast characters, keep your aim ready and attack after they finish a dash or transformation. Against ranged attacks, move unpredictably and avoid standing still. Learn when each opponent is vulnerable.
